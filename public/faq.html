Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our food truck, menu, and how to order from Taco Tom's.

Where is Taco Tom's located?

We’re based in Vancouver, WA. Our lunch truck is usually parked at our regular spot on 13510 NE Fourth Plain Blvd Vancouver, WA 98682. Check the Hours page for our current schedule and any special event days.

What kind of food do you serve?

We focus on fresh, made‑to‑order Mexican favorites: tacos, burritos, quesadillas, tortas, carne asada fries, and more. You can see the full lineup on our Menu page.

Do you offer vegetarian or gluten‑friendly options?

Yes. We have veggie options and can adjust many items on request. Ask at the truck or check the menu for notes on vegetarian and gluten‑friendly choices.

How can I place an order online?

You can order through DoorDash using the Order Now button in the navigation or directly from our online ordering link. For large orders or catering, please contact us.

Do you cater events?

Yes, we offer on‑site catering in the Vancouver area. Instead of bringing the full truck, our team arrives with a portable grill and all the ingredients, then cooks and serves fresh food at your event. Tell us about your event (date, location, guest count) on the Contact page, and we’ll follow up with pricing and menu options.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and major cards at the truck. For delivery orders, payment is handled through DoorDash.
